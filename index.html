Castle Hills ER

24/7 Freestanding Emergency Room in Carrollton, TX

Emergency Care, Close to Home

Castle Hills ER is a freestanding emergency room operated by Immediate Care Centers of Texas at 4228 N Josey Ln, Carrollton, TX 75010. The facility is open 24 hours a day, seven days a week, and is staffed by board-certified physicians around the clock.

What to Expect

  • Board-certified emergency physicians on-site at all times
  • No-wait positioning: most patients seen within 20 minutes of arrival
  • Most major insurance plans accepted
  • Full range of emergency conditions treated, the same as a hospital-based ER

Part of a Larger Network

Castle Hills ER is one of three Immediate Care Centers of Texas locations in the Dallas-Fort Worth area, alongside Frisco ER and Urgent Care of Plano. Every location shares the same standard of board-certified care and no-wait positioning.
